Privacy Notice

How 852 Technologies handles information associated with this corporate website.

Scope

852 Technologies is a Pennsylvania-based business. This Privacy Notice applies to the 852 Technologies corporate website at https://852-technologies.github.io/web/.

Each 852 Technologies application or service may have its own privacy policy governing that product. This notice does not replace those product-specific policies.

Information you provide

If you contact us through our inquiry form, you may voluntarily provide information such as:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your company or organization
  • Details about your inquiry or requested service
  • Any other information included in your message

You are not required to provide this information, but we may be unable to respond without sufficient contact details.

Website usage information

The website is hosted through GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information needed to deliver and secure the website, such as an IP address, device information, requested pages, referring website, and request time. More information is available in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

We use GoatCounter to measure aggregate website activity. GoatCounter does not use cookies, local storage, advertising identifiers, or cross-site tracking. According to GoatCounter, it does not store visitors’ IP addresses, full user-agent strings, or tracker IDs. It may produce aggregate statistics such as page views, referring pages, browser type, operating system, approximate location, language, and screen size. More information is available in GoatCounter’s privacy documentation.

How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries and service requests
  • Evaluate potential projects or business relationships
  • Maintain relevant business correspondence
  • Operate, secure, and improve the website
  • Understand aggregate website usage
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

We do not sell personal information or use it for cross-site behavioral advertising.

Service providers

Our website and inquiry process use third-party service providers, including GitHub Pages for hosting, GoatCounter for privacy-oriented aggregate analytics, and OpnForm for processing inquiry-form submissions.

These providers may process information in locations outside Pennsylvania or the United States. Their handling of information is also governed by their respective privacy policies. See the OpnForm Privacy Policy.

Retention

We retain inquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage a potential or existing business relationship, maintain appropriate business records, or satisfy legal obligations. Aggregate website statistics may be retained for website analysis and improvement.

Privacy choices and requests

You may ask to access, correct, or delete personal information you previously submitted. Some information may be retained when reasonably necessary for legal, security, or recordkeeping purposes. Send privacy requests to support@852technologies.com.

Browser privacy signals

This website does not use cross-site behavioral tracking or sell personal information. Because no tracking-based sale or advertising activity occurs, browser “Do Not Track” and Global Privacy Control signals do not change the website’s operation.

Children’s privacy

The corporate website is intended for a general business audience and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

External websites and applications

The website contains links to application websites and third-party services. Their privacy practices are governed by their own notices. The privacy policies for individual 852 Technologies applications remain available on their respective websites, including Itemity and eMatch Lite.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures intended to protect information. However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Changes to this notice

We may update this Privacy Notice as our website or practices change. The effective date at the top identifies the latest version.
